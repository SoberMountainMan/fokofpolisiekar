Tydlyn

2003–2026 — twee dekades van Afrikaanse rock.

2003
Band formed in Bellville in April — initially as a joke, the idea of an Afrikaans punk band being ridiculous at the time. Debut EP As Jy Met Vuur Speel Sal Jy Brand released on Punkskelm Records.
2004
Debut studio album Lugsteuring released. Recorded at B-Sharp Studios.
2005
Monoloog In Stereo EP released — 6 tracks showing songwriting maturation.
Feb 2006
Drummer Jaco Venter jumps out of the band's moving tour van — the start of a turbulent year.
Mar 2006
Blasphemy firestorm: Christian groups cry foul after a band member writes “F*k God” on a fan's wallet (reported by IOL).
2006
Studio album Swanesang released (12 tracks), plus the Brand Suid-Afrika CD single.
2006–2007
Hiatus — “it felt like nothing was working.” Members form Van Coke Kartel, Die Heuwels Fantasties and aKING.
2008
Antibiotika EP released — 4 new tracks and 8 music videos.
2009
Documentary Forgive Them For They Know Not What They Do (dir. Bryan Little) premieres at Encounters and wins the Audience Award. Later: Showmax (2017); IMDb 8.8.
2011
Annie Klopper's book Biografie van 'n Bende: Die Storie van Fokofpolisiekar published (Protea Boekhuis).
2012
10-year anniversary. Fokofpolisiekar 10 Year Anniversary compilation + DVD released.
2017
Selfmedikasie released — third studio album, first in 11 years. Crowdfunded via Thundafund, hitting the R500,000 target in 9 days.
2018
Droom Hoog EP released (5 tracks).
2020
Lockdown EP Kajuitkoors (cabin fever) — 4 tracks in 12 minutes — plus the first Unplugged livestream in November.
2021
The Sound of Fokof Unplugged live album released.
2023
Dans Deur Die Donker album (12 tracks) — and the 20-jaar celebrations: anniversary vinyl restoring the original debut artwork, plus the full-catalog cassette box set.
2026
New album in progress — working title Post-Afrikaner. Swanesang turns 20 with an AU/NZ tour.

Die Storie

The story behind the museum.

Die Storie / The Story:

Ons het die domein gesien en gekoop om dit terug te gee. Die band het gesê: “Ons is 100% homegrown .co.za — maar maak daarvan ’n museum.”

En dit is presies wat ons gedoen het.

We saw the domain and bought it to give it back. The band said: “We’re 100% homegrown .co.za — but make it a museum.” So that’s exactly what we did.

DIE BAND

Fokofpolisiekar is 'n Afrikaanse rock-band vanuit Bellville, Wes-Kaap. Aktief sedert 2003. Sewe ateljeealbums. Die stem van 'n generasie.

Hierdie webwerf is 'n onafhanklike fan-argief. Nie geaffilieer met, goedgekeur deur, of verbonde aan Fokofpolisiekar, hul bestuur, of hul plate-etiket nie.

Alle handelsmerke, album-kunswerk, en musiek behoort aan die onderskeie eienaars. Hierdie site huisves geen kopieregmateriaal nie — alle skakels verwys na amptelike kanale.

💡 Pasella Kennisgewing: Alle skakels is pasella. 0% kommissie. 100% van strome en verkeer gaan direk na die band en amptelike kanale.
