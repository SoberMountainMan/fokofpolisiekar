Tydlyn

2003–2026 — twee dekades van Afrikaanse rock.

2003
Band formed in Bellville, near Cape Town. Debut EP As Jy Met Vuur Speel Sal Jy Brand released on Punkskelm Records.
2004
Debut studio album Lugsteuring released. Recorded at B-Sharp Studios.
2005
Monoloog In Stereo EP released — 6 tracks showing songwriting maturation.
2006
Studio album Swanesang released (12 tracks). Christian groups accused the band of blasphemy; controversy reported by IOL.
2006–2007
Band went on hiatus. Members formed side projects: Van Coke Kartel, Die Heuwels Fantasties, aKING.
2008
Antibiotika EP released — double CD with 4 new tracks and 8 music videos.
2009
Documentary Forgive Them For They Know Not What They Do premiered at Encounters Film Festival.
2012
10-year anniversary. Fokofpolisiekar 10 Year Anniversary compilation released.
2017
Selfmedikasie released — third studio album, first in 11 years. Crowdfunded via Thundafund, hitting R500,000 target in 9 days.
2018
Droom Hoog EP released (5 tracks).
2023
Dans Deur Die Donker album released (12 tracks) on Punkskelm Records.
2026
New album in progress. Working title: Post-Afrikaner (originally a ‘Monoloog 2’ EP concept). Targeted release ~October 2026.

Alle feite uit openbare bronne. Fout? Laat weet ons.

Die Storie

The story behind the museum.

Die Storie / The Story:

Ons het die domein gekoop om dit terug te gee. Hulle het gesê: “Laat hom gaan, ons is 100% homegrown .co.za.”

Toe verander ons hom maar in ’n museum.

We bought the domain to give it back. They said: “Let it go, we’re 100% homegrown .co.za.” So we turned it into a museum.

DIE BAND

Fokofpolisiekar is 'n Afrikaanse rock-band vanuit Bellville, Wes-Kaap. Aktief sedert 2003. Sewe ateljeealbums. Die stem van 'n generasie.

Hierdie webwerf is 'n onafhanklike fan-argief. Nie geaffilieer met, goedgekeur deur, of verbonde aan Fokofpolisiekar, hul bestuur, of hul plate-etiket nie.

Alle handelsmerke, album-kunswerk, en musiek behoort aan die onderskeie eienaars. Hierdie site huisves geen kopieregmateriaal nie — alle skakels verwys na amptelike kanale.

💡 Pasella Kennisgewing: Alle skakels is pasella. 0% kommissie. 100% van strome en verkeer gaan direk na die band en amptelike kanale.
