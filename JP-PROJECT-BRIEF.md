# fokofpolisiekar.com — Die Informele Museum

## Project Brief for JP

---

## 1. The Vision

**Die Informele Museum** is an unofficial fan archive celebrating 20+ years of Fokofpolisiekar and South African rock history.

The story writes itself: *"Ons het die domein gekoop om dit terug te gee. Hulle het gesê: 'Laat hom gaan, ons is 100% homegrown .co.za.' Toe verander ons hom maar in 'n museum."*

Every link on the site points to official band channels. Zero commercial intent. 100% pasella.

---

## 2. What's on the Site

### Hero + Origin Story
The "Gooi Man" backstory, the tagline *"Some revolutions are worth keeping alive..."*, and the legendary *As Jy Met Vuur Speel* (2003) EP artwork framed like a museum artifact. Visitors land here and immediately understand what this is — a love letter to the band.

### Pasella Manifesto
A clear, visible notice: **0% revenue cut. All links go directly to the band and official channels.** Every stream, every ticket sale, every rand — straight to the artists. No middlemen.

---

## 3. What's NOT on the Site

This matters. The site is designed to be legally bulletproof:

- **No merch store** — all merch links go to the official .co.za shop
- **No hosted music files or videos** — everything streams via Spotify and YouTube embeds
- **No copyrighted band imagery hosted directly** — only small thumbnails and embedded players
- **No commercial intent whatsoever** — the site makes zero rand
- **Clear "unofficial fan archive" disclaimer** visible on every page
