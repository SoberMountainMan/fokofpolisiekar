## IMPORTANT: Album Name Corrections

The project brief (JP-PROJECT-BRIEF.md) lists 7 albums. **Three names do not match the actual releases:**

| Brief says | Actual title | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| Monoloog in Stereofonie (2005) | **Monoloog In Stereo** | Stereofonie is not the real title |
| Swart Melders (2006) | **Swanesang** (2006) | Swart Melders does not exist in the Fokofpolisiekar discography. No such album found on Spotify, Apple Music, Discogs, or Wikipedia |
| Droom Hoog (2023) | **Droom Hoog** is a 2018 EP; the 2023 album is **Dans Deur Die Donker** | Wrong year and wrong release type |

The actual Fokofpolisiekar studio album discography (per Wikipedia, Spotify, Discogs) is:
1. Lugsteuring (2004)
2. Swanesang (2006)
3. Selfmedikasie (2017)
4. Dans Deur Die Donker (2023)
5. Post-Afrikaner (upcoming, per Wikipedia)

## Section 3: Gaps / Unverifiable

The following commonly-cited band facts could NOT be verified from 2+ public sources and should be OMITTED or flagged on the timeline:

1. **Swart Melders album** - No trace of this title anywhere in the Fokofpolisiekar discography. Not on Spotify, Apple Music, Discogs, Wikipedia, or any news source. This appears to be an error in the project brief. The 2006 album is Swanesang.

2. **Witbank wall incident** - Could not find any verifiable public source for a specific Witbank wall incident. There was a 2006 blasphemy controversy (wallet incident, per IOL), and Hunter Kennedy mentioned a Potchefstroom incident with a man in an apartheid flag t-shirt (Backseat Mafia), but no specific Witbank wall event was found.

3. **Exact formation month** - Apple Music says April 2003; Spotify bio says August 2003; fokofpolisiekar.co.za says just 2003. These conflict. Recommend stating 2003 without a specific month, or noting the discrepancy.

4. **Exact crowdfunding amount raised** - Bizcommunity says they exceeded their dream goal of R500,000 in 9 days. The fokofpolisiekar.co.za/fund page shows R262,350 raised towards funding a new album (possibly a different campaign or a snapshot at a different time). The exact final total is unclear.

5. **Droom Hoog as a 2023 album** - The project brief lists Droom Hoog (2023) but Droom Hoog is a 2018 EP. The 2023 album is Dans Deur Die Donker. These are different releases.

6. **Specific album release dates (day/month)** - Only year-level dates are reliably verifiable for most releases. Exceptions: Antibiotika (June 1, 2008 per Apple Music/Amazon), Dans Deur Die Donker single (November 23, 2023 per Apple Music), album (December 7, 2023 per Shazam/Genius).

7. **Annie Kloppers book Biografie van n bende** - Referenced in the project brief but not used as a source per the task instructions. Could not independently verify specific claims from this book against other sources.

8. **Oppikoppi performance year** - Hunter Kennedy mentions first Oppikoppi show in the Backseat Mafia interview but does not specify the year. Could not cross-reference.

9. **EU tour dates** - Referenced by Hunter Kennedy (The EU tour will always stick with me) but no specific dates or sources found.

10. **Post-Afrikaner (2026) release** - Listed on Wikipedia as upcoming but not yet released. Instagram posts reference it as a working title. Cannot verify release date or confirm it will be released.
